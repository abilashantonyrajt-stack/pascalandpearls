## Name
Pascal & Pearls — Artisan Handcrafted Jewelry

## Tagline
Timeless elegance, handmade with passion. Discover handcrafted pearl jewelry and beaded sets.

## Description
Pascal & Pearls is an artisan jewelry brand offering handcrafted beaded jewelry and artificial pearl sets. Every piece is designed with care and crafted by skilled artisans.

Features:
- 🛍️ Browse collections of necklaces, earrings, bracelets, and bridal sets
- 📱 Mobile-friendly PWA — add to home screen for app-like experience
- 🔐 Secure checkout with UPI & Cash on Delivery
- 🎁 Coupon codes & wishlist
- 📦 Real-time order tracking
- 💬 WhatsApp support

Built with Next.js 16, Firebase, and Tailwind CSS.
